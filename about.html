About us

We started The Sourcers because we were tired of juggling four vendors to get one container home. Today we are that one team for hundreds of brands.

Our story

One team from the first factory visit to the last mile.

Most importers work with a sourcing agent, an inspector, a forwarder and a customs broker. When a shipment slips, each one points at the next. We built a company where that conversation can’t happen, because all four jobs sit with the same people.

Our teams in Karachi, Shenzhen and Ho Chi Minh City visit factories, check production, book space and clear your goods, sharing one system and one set of targets: your stock, on time, as specified.
